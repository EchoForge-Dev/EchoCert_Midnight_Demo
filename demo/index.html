      THE SAME CREDENTIAL, TWO WAYS
      
    

    

      

        
        
        BEFORE
        
EchoCert on Cardano

        
A public credential registry, live today. Anyone can verify a diploma — and
           anyone can read every field of it. Verifiability came at the price of privacy.

      

      

        
        
        AFTER
        
EchoCert on Midnight

        
The same credential, proved one field at a time. The verifier learns that the
           diploma is real and nothing else — and cannot link two proofs to one holder.
