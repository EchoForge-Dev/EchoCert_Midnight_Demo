# Title cards — prompt for Claude Design

Three 16:9 cards for the video: shot 2 (BEFORE hook), shot 9 (three universities),
shot 9b (closing). Paste the block below into Claude Design as one request, then
export each artboard as a 1920×1080 PNG. Shot 6's "Landing on Midnight (sped up)"
is a lower-third added in the editor, not a card.

One deliberate change from VIDEO-SCRIPT v4: the "288 bytes" figure is dropped —
it was never measured on this build, and a number on screen invites a question.

---

Design three 1920×1080 title cards for a video. They must read as one system.
Rules, no exceptions:

- Background pure black #000000. Text pure white #FFFFFF and gray #808080 only.
  One exception: a single small green dot #4EDE80 may appear on card 3.
- Typeface: IBM Plex Mono for everything. Labels are uppercase with 0.15em
  letter-spacing (at 1080p: labels ~22px, body ~34px, headline ~80–96px).
  Regular and medium weights only — no bold display faces, no italics.
- Every card has four thin engineering corner markers: 1px lines, gray at 30%
  opacity, 24px L-shapes, marking a frame inset 48px from the edges.
- Zero decoration: no gradients, no rounded boxes, no icons, no illustrations,
  no photos, no logos. Only text, 1px rules, and the corner markers.
- Left-aligned text, generous negative space. Small label top-left; headline in
  the lower-left third.

Card 1 — BEFORE
Top-left: a solid white tag with black uppercase text "BEFORE", 0.18em tracking,
padding 8px 16px.
Small gray label beneath it: ECHOCERT ON CARDANO · PUBLIC REGISTRY
Headline, white, two lines:
  Midnight University.
  Two applicants.
Body, gray, two lines:
  Chuck's diploma is already on EchoCert — public on Cardano, verifiable by anyone.
  Admissions needs one field. The registry shows everything.
Leave the right half (a 960×540 area, vertically centred) empty: a screenshot of
the real product will be placed there in the edit.

Card 2 — THREE UNIVERSITIES
Top-left gray label: [ 09 ] UNLINKABLE
Three equal columns separated by 1px vertical gray rules. Each column has an
uppercase gray label at the top — MIDNIGHT UNIVERSITY / CARDANO UNIVERSITY /
ECHOFORGE UNIVERSITY — and beneath it the same line in white:
  ✓ BSc Computer Science
Draw nothing between the columns. No arrows, no connectors, no shared line —
the absence of any connection is the point of the card.
Below the columns, white headline: None of them can tell.
Gray body, three lines:
  EchoCert on Cardano is the public registry. EchoCert on Midnight is the private proof.
  Same product. One new line.
  One proof, checked by math — and it can't be used to track who applied where.

Card 3 — CLOSING
Only two lines of text, vertically centred, left-aligned at the left third:
  The forger is real.                       (gray)
  He built the system that caught him.      (white, larger)
Add nothing else to this card: no name, no date, no age, no number, no logo.

Export each artboard as PNG, 1920×1080.
